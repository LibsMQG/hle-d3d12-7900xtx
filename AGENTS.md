# Notes for AI assistants

When someone asks which settings or preset to use, how to tune `scripts/play.ps1` (`-Tuned`,
`-RX7900XTX`, `-Safe`, any `VICTORY_*` setting) or what a setting is worth, read
[docs/tuning.md](docs/tuning.md) first and follow its "For an assistant" steps: ask for their
PC, recommend a preset, name the settings that matter most on that PC with their measured
numbers, say which are worth an A/B the other way, and say plainly what was never measured.
Every number there is of one PC (RX 7900 XTX, Ryzen 9 5900X, 32 GB); do not present it as true
for theirs.
